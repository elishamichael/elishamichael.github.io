Problems I help with

What B2B technology companies are struggling with in 2026

Current industry evidence, my approach, and the experience behind it.

Retention

Retention is under pressure

Median net revenue retention102%
Bottom quartile 92% · median gross retention 84%
Aleph & Benchmarkit, 2026 [1] · 342 companies, 2025 data

My approach: Rank accounts by risk and agree renewal plans early. Give every key account an executive sponsor.

My experience: 98% renewal collection on 700+ accounts (PowerSchool); churn-risk criteria and long-term renewals (Apptio).
Data

Health scores not trusted or used

Customer data fragmented52.1%
Health scores reviewed ad hoc or never60.7%
2026 State of Customer Success, via CS Collective [2] · sample size not stated

My approach: Agree a short list of health signals the team trusts. Build one account view with a fixed review rhythm.

My experience: health scoring and SLA tracking in Salesforce and Gainsight (PowerSchool).
Alignment

CS and Sales on different numbers

CS teams sharing NRR with Sales17.3%
2026 State of Customer Success, via CS Collective [2]

My approach: Keep one shared renewal and expansion forecast. Make handoffs clear from onboarding to renewal to growth.

My experience: growth opportunities coordinated with Sales; onboarding handoffs owned (Apptio).
AI cost

The full cost of AI is not visible

Not capturing the full hidden costs of AI78%
TBM Council pulse survey [3] · 640 responses, updated 23 Oct 2026

My approach: Cost AI beyond tokens: people, data, integration and compliance. Show leaders the full lifecycle cost.

My experience: TBM roadmaps and use cases with C-level customers (Apptio). AI cost is the newest chapter of the same work.
Funding

Leaders and practitioners disagree

Practitioners: no standard AI funding model40%
Executives: believe mature mechanisms exist72%
TBM Council, 2026 pulse surveys [4]

My approach: Give IT, finance and the business the same numbers. Start with showback, then move to chargeback.

My experience: executive quarterly reviews for 18 accounts (Apptio).
Shadow tech

Tools growing outside central IT

Practitioners: IT supports 7+ AI tools55%
Executives: believe 6 or fewer55%
TBM Council [4]

My approach: Keep one registry of tools and one intake route. Require a business case before funding.

My experience: data models, Jira/ServiceNow/ERP integrations and executive dashboards (Targetprocess).
